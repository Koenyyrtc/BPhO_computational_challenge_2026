# Hydrogen Emission Spectrum — How It Works

## Physics

When a hydrogen electron drops from level **n₂** to **n₁** (n₂ > n₁), it emits a photon. The Bohr model predicts its wavelength and energy:

**Rydberg formula (wavelength):**
$$\frac{1}{\lambda} = R_H \left(\frac{1}{n_1^2} - \frac{1}{n_2^2}\right)$$

**Photon energy:**
$$E = R_H \cdot hc \left(\frac{1}{n_1^2} - \frac{1}{n_2^2}\right)$$

| Symbol | Value | Meaning |
|--------|-------|---------|
| R_H | 1.097 × 10⁷ m⁻¹ | Rydberg constant |
| h | 6.626 × 10⁻³⁴ J·s | Planck's constant |
| c | 3 × 10⁸ m/s | Speed of light |

## Spectral Series

Each series groups transitions to the same lower level n₁:

| Series | n₁ | Region |
|--------|----|--------|
| Lyman | 1 | UV |
| Balmer | 2 | Visible |
| Paschen | 3 | Near-IR |
| Brackett | 4 | IR |
| Pfund | 5 | IR |
